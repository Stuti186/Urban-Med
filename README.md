# 🏥 UrbanMed-Sync

##  Project Overview

**UrbanMed Sync** is an innovative hospital management solution designed to streamline patient management in **Outpatient Departments (OPDs)**, optimize **bed availability**, and enhance the **admission process**.

Built using advanced **queuing models**, the system helps hospitals manage patient flow efficiently, reduce waiting times, and improve overall healthcare delivery.

The solution also incorporates a robust **inventory management module** to track the usage and availability of medicines and consumables. It helps hospitals maintain optimal stock levels, preventing both shortages and unnecessary overstocking.

---

##  Key Features

### Advanced Queuing Models

- Implements queuing algorithms to efficiently manage patient flow in OPDs.
- Helps reduce patient waiting times.
- Ensures timely and organized healthcare delivery.

### Bed Availability Tracking

- Real-time monitoring of bed occupancy.
- Tracks available and occupied beds across hospital departments.
- Helps hospital staff make faster admission decisions.

### Patient Admission Management

- Streamlines the patient admission process.
- Enables quick and accurate patient onboarding.
- Reduces manual paperwork and processing time.

### Inventory Management

- Tracks medicines and medical consumables.
- Monitors stock usage and availability.
- Provides alerts for low-stock items.
- Helps prevent medicine shortages and overstocking.

### City-Wide Integration

- Designed to integrate with city-wide healthcare modules.
- Enables hospitals to participate in a unified healthcare management ecosystem.
- Provides scalability for future expansion.

### User-Friendly Interface

- Clean and intuitive interface.
- Easy navigation for hospital staff.
- Minimal training required for users.

---

## Purpose

The primary goal of **UrbanMed-Sync** is to modernize and digitize hospital operations, making healthcare services more **efficient, organized, and accessible**.

Although the initial implementation focuses on individual hospitals, the system is designed with **scalability** in mind and can potentially be integrated into a **city-wide healthcare management system**.
